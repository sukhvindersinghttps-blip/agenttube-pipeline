# 07 — Metadata & SEO

Every upload ships with full packaging. No bare uploads.

## Required on every video

- **Title** — follows the channel's winning formula (see [Analytics](09-analytics.md)); never flat declarative unless the formula calls for it.
- **Description** — opens with 2 keyword-rich lines, then chapters.
- **Chapters** — on every upload, including back-catalog when touched.
- **Subtitles** — SRT uploaded separately (never burned-in unless the format demands it).
- **End screen** — points at the channel's proven winner until 2+ winners exist.

## Engagement

- **Pinned comment** within 1 hour of publish: *"🔥 [Question]? Drop your pick below — **[A]** or **[B]**? I'll pin the best argument. 👇"*
- Note: the YouTube Data API has no pin-comment endpoint — pinning is always a manual step in YouTube Studio.
