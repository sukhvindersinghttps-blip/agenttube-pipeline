# 09 — Analytics-Driven Strategy

## 72-hour judgment rule

At T+72h after publish, pull views:

| Views | Verdict | Action |
|-------|---------|--------|
| < 50 | **Flatlined** | No re-edits, no re-promotion. Log it, move on. |
| 50–200 | **Watch** | Re-check at day 7. |
| > 200 | **Winner** | Greenlight a sequel as the channel's next upload. |

## Sequel strategy

- A winner's **next upload is a direct sequel** in the same winning formula + a new broad topic.
- Point every new upload's end screen at the proven winner until 2+ winners exist.
- Build series playlists around winning formulas.
- A channel needs 3–4 winners to be stable.

## Winning title formulas (rotate within; new formats need human approval)

| Channel lane | Formula |
|--------------|---------|
| History | "The Greatest [X] That Never Was" (or curiosity-gap question — never flat declarative) |
| Psychology | "How to Trick Your Brain Into [benefit]" |
| War stories | "[N] Forbidden [elite group] Tricks That [outcome]" / "He [impossible feat] — [twist]" |

## Comment CTA (every video)

- Closing narration beat: *"So — [binary question]? Tell me in the comments."*
- Pinned comment within 1h (see [Metadata](07-metadata.md)).
